# Deferred learning and follow-up

This is a temporary action queue, not a history log or permanent backlog. Every item has one owner, a concrete next action, and a calendar due date/time no later than seven calendar days after creation or the next milestone review, whichever comes first. Process, verify, move the durable result to its authoritative home, then delete the item here. If there is nothing deferred, keep this file empty or omit it.

## Entry template — duplicate only for genuinely deferred items

### [Short, newcomer-readable description of the issue]

- **Situation:** What happened, explained without unexplained project shorthand.
- **Expected behavior:** What should have happened and why.
- **Significance:** Why this matters; consequence or recurrence risk.
- **Cause:** Best current causal explanation; label uncertainty honestly.
- **Responsible layer:** The specific layer that owns the cause (for example: assignment, design, verification, role instruction, evaluation, or runtime).
- **Owner:** One accountable owner.
- **Next action:** A concrete action and intended permanent destination.
- **Verification:** Evidence that will demonstrate the correction works.
- **Due point:** Specific calendar date/time; no later than seven calendar days after creation or the next milestone review, whichever comes first.
- **Evidence:** Links to the exact supporting artifacts/logs/tests. Do not make the reader reconstruct the explanation from these links.
