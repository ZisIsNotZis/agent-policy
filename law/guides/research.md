# Research

## Before gathering

State the question, the decision it serves, and what result would settle it. A question with no deciding observation is reading, not research.

## Primary first

Follow a claim to the source that owns it, and record its version and date. A summary is evidence about the summary.

Keep the negative result. A closed path is a finding, and deleting it makes the next node walk it again.

## Evaluation

Fix the population, the split, the metric and the baseline before looking at results. Keep the held-out set out of selection. State the budget of the thing being compared, not only its quality.

## Record

Bind a run to its revision, configuration, environment and seed. One run supports only what its variance allows. Compare resource use alongside the claim.
